About VoiceDraft

VoiceDraft is a free, browser-based voice transcription tool. Speak into your microphone and your words are transcribed in real-time using your browser's built-in speech recognition — no accounts, no servers, no fees.

All transcripts are saved automatically to your browser's local storage. Nothing leaves your device.

  • Real-time voice-to-text
  • 13+ supported languages
  • Export as TXT, Markdown, HTML, or JSON
  • Multiple saved sessions
  • 100% offline capable after first load

Powered by the Web Speech API — works best in Chrome and Edge.
