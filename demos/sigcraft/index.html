How to add your signature

📧 Gmail

  1. Click "Copy HTML"
  2. Open Gmail → Settings
  3. Click "See all settings"
  4. Scroll to Signature
  5. Click in the editor
  6. Paste (Ctrl+V)

💙 Outlook

  1. Click "Copy HTML"
  2. Open Outlook → Settings
  3. Go to Mail → Compose
  4. Click "Email signature"
  5. Edit signature block
  6. Paste (Ctrl+V)

🍎 Apple Mail

  1. Click "Copy HTML"
  2. Open Mail → Preferences
  3. Click Signatures
  4. Create new signature
  5. Paste into field
  6. Assign to account

🏢 Any Client

  1. Click "Copy HTML"
  2. Find your email app's
  3. signature settings
  4. Look for "HTML" mode
  5. Paste the code
  6. Save & enjoy!
